The Hanging Church

  1. Home
  2. Destination
  3. History
  4. The Hanging Church

The Hanging Church, known as Saint Virgin Mary's Coptic Orthodox Church, is one of Cairo's oldest and most famous churches. Located in the Coptic Cairo district, it is built atop the remains of the ancient Babylon Fortress, giving it a unique elevated appearance that has earned it the name "Hanging." Dating back to the 3rd century AD, the church features a stunning blend of architectural styles, showcasing intricate woodwork, beautiful icons, and detailed frescoes. Its interior is adorned with a wooden ceiling resembling the hull of a ship, symbolizing the Ark of Noah. The Hanging Church holds significant importance for Coptic Christians and is dedicated to the Virgin Mary. It has been a center of worship and pilgrimage for centuries, and its serene atmosphere invites contemplation. Visitors can admire the church’s historical artifacts and experience its spiritual ambiance, making it a must-visit site for anyone interested in Egypt's rich religious heritage.
