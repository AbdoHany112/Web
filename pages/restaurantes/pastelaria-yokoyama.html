"In the working class neighborhood of Rod El Farag, you’ll know Sobhy Kaber by the crowd. The restaurant is ample in size, but don’t be surprised if you find yourself waiting for a table, especially during peak hours, since it attracts a diverse array of Egyptians and food enthusiasts. The extensive menu features dishes such as mombar (sausage casings stuffed with herby spiced rice), the traditional Egyptian staple molokhia (rich green mallow soup), hamam mahshi (fried pigeon stuffed with seasoned rice and gizzards), and a variety of grilled meats. Despite the restaurant’s location in a neighborhood not typically visited by tourists, exuberant staff keep Sobhy Kaber fun and inviting for visitors"

Ahmed Mostafa

"A Real Taste of Cairo!" I’ve heard about Sobhy Kaber for years and finally decided to visit last Friday. The place was packed, but the wait was totally worth it. I started with their famous mombar, which was seasoned perfectly—crispy on the outside, soft and flavorful on the inside. The molokhia reminded me of my grandmother’s cooking, and the grilled kofta was simply divine. Sobhy Kaber is where you go for authentic Egyptian street food, and despite the crowds, the staff were incredibly friendly and kept the energy up. Highly recommend for anyone craving real Egyptian flavor

Yasmine El Shamy

"Great Food but Be Prepared to Wait!" I went to Sobhy Kaber with my family last Saturday, and while the food was excellent, the wait time was pretty long. We had to wait about 45 minutes to get a table, but once we sat down, the food came fast. The highlight of the night for me was the hamam mahshi; it was juicy, crispy, and stuffed with rice that had the perfect balance of spices. The portions are generous, so don’t worry about leaving hungry! The service was good, but the crowd made it hard to enjoy the atmosphere fully. Definitely worth trying if you’re a fan of traditional Egyptian food

Khaled Ibrahim

"Best Grilled Meat in Cairo!" Sobhy Kaber is hands down the best place in Cairo for grilled meats. I’ve been coming here for years, and they never disappoint. The lamb chops are always cooked to perfection, tender and flavorful, and the mix grill platter is my go-to order. It’s in Rod El Farag, so if you’re not familiar with the area, it might seem a bit off the beaten path, but the vibe is great once you’re inside. The staff is lively, and they make sure you have a good time. Whether you’re going with friends or family, you’ll leave satisfied
