"Located in the heart of Cairo’s Garden City neighborhood, Escobar attracts both locals and tourists for its spectacular views of the Nile from its towering location high over the city. Though the restaurant does offer a diverse menu, the food isn’t the main draw; stick to the extensive selection of beer, wine, and in-house cocktails. It’s an ideal spot for large groups, but you’ll want to make a reservation in advance, especially in the evening"

Hany George

"Stunning Nile Views and a Laid-Back Vibe" Escobar is hands down one of the coolest spots in Cairo if you're looking for great drinks and even better views. The towering location in Garden City gives you an unbeatable view of the Nile, especially at sunset. I went with friends, and it’s perfect for large groups. Their beer selection was impressive, and I absolutely loved their in-house cocktails—the bartender really knows what they’re doing. The food isn’t the main attraction, but it’s decent if you’re just there for drinks. Make sure to book ahead, especially if you’re heading there in the evening!

Khalil Osama

"Best Place for Drinks and Nile Views" I had an amazing evening at Escobar with a group of friends. The views of the Nile from up high are stunning, and the whole atmosphere is relaxed yet vibrant. I stuck with their selection of wine and cocktails, and I wasn’t disappointed—the flavors were unique and refreshing. Although they have food on the menu, I’d say this is more of a place to enjoy drinks with friends while soaking in the breathtaking scenery. Definitely make a reservation if you’re going at night; it gets busy fast. Escobar is perfect for a casual hangout or a special evening out in Cairo.

Ahmed Farouk

"Great Drinks and Even Greater Views" Escobar’s location is incredible—you feel like you’re floating over the Nile with how high up it is. I went there with some friends, and we had a great time sampling their beer and cocktail selection. The drinks were well-made, especially their signature cocktails. The food is fine, but honestly, Escobar is more of a place to enjoy a drink while taking in the incredible views. The space is perfect for larger groups, but definitely make a reservation if you want to secure a table. It’s become one of my favorite spots in Cairo for a laid-back evening with a great view!
