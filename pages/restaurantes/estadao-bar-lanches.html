Crimson Bar & Grill

"When residents of densely packed Zamalek aren’t busy sipping coffee in one of the island’s compact cafes, shopping in its boutiques, or perusing its art galleries, they head for Crimson’s relaxed terrace for a breezy breakfast or a sophisticated night out. A fantastic view of the Nile is the perfect backdrop for stylish Mediterranean and Italian dishes. Start with the salmon roll (beetroot-cured salmon with cream cheese and shrimp, topped with salty black caviar), followed by the succulent grilled chateaubriand for two, and the signature pineapple creme brulee (baked within a pineapple). Crimson is also known for its impressive selection of cocktails, most notably a bright gin and tonic infused with coriander and rosemary. Reservations are highly recommended"

Omar Saleh

"Mediterranean Elegance with a View" Crimson Bar & Grill in Zamalek is a hidden gem for anyone who loves Mediterranean and Italian cuisine. I loved sitting on the terrace, soaking in the view of the Nile while enjoying my meal. The salmon roll was a great starter—delicate flavors of beetroot-cured salmon, paired with shrimp and cream cheese, all topped with black caviar. My partner and I shared the chateaubriand, and it was absolutely delicious, juicy, and flavorful. We ended our meal with the pineapple creme brulee, which was not only tasty but served in a pineapple, making it both eye-catching and indulgent. I also tried their signature gin and tonic infused with rosemary—it was incredibly refreshing. Crimson is a perfect spot for a classy night out

Asmaa Nazih

"A Sophisticated Escape in Zamalek" Crimson Bar & Grill is my go-to spot whenever I want to unwind in Zamalek. The terrace offers an unbeatable view of the Nile, perfect for a relaxing evening. I started with the salmon roll—the combination of beetroot-cured salmon, cream cheese, and shrimp was divine, especially with the touch of black caviar. The highlight of the night, though, was the grilled chateaubriand. It was cooked to perfection, and the portion for two was generous. The pineapple creme brulee was a unique and delicious end to the meal, especially with its creative presentation inside a pineapple. The cocktails were equally amazing—the gin and tonic with coriander and rosemary had such a refreshing twist. Highly recommend reserving a table!
